# SI404

### Super Intelligence × Community Intelligence

**SI404** is an open experiment exploring what happens when human intelligence, community participation, and AI-assisted reasoning come together.

The project begins with a simple question:

> **Can a community become more intelligent when humans and AI solve problems together?**

---

## 🧠 Intelligence Arena

The **Intelligence Arena** is a proposed environment where humans and AI can participate in structured challenges involving:

- Reasoning
- Research
- Prediction
- Problem solving
- AI-assisted analysis
- Community intelligence

The objective is not simply to ask AI for answers.

The objective is to explore whether **AI + humans + collective intelligence** can produce better outcomes together.

---

## 🔬 The Experiment

SI404 is being built as an evolving experiment.

Ideas will be proposed, tested, measured and improved through community participation.

Development will progressively explore:

- Intelligence Arena
- Community challenges
- AI-assisted research
- Collective decision systems
- Reputation and contribution mechanisms
- Experimental intelligence tools

Features may evolve based on technical feasibility, testing and community feedback.
